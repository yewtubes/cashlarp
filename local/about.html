About Cashlarp

Cashlarp is a local roleplay app using a captured web client and an independent backend. Balances, payments, Bitcoin and cards are simulated. No bank account, payment network or cryptocurrency network is connected.

Cashlarp is not affiliated with Cash App or Block. Captured client software and original artwork belong to their respective owners. The Cashlarp identity is a separate project brand.

The default Bitcoin quote is fixed at $100,000/BTC. The captured holdings toggle shows satoshis: 100,000,000 satoshis equals one Bitcoin.
